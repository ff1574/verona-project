Travel Tip Important

Many churches and museums close for lunch (roughly 12:30-3:00 PM), so plan accordingly. Most shops also close on Sunday afternoons and Monday mornings.

Featured Attraction

The Arena di Verona is one of the best-preserved ancient Roman amphitheatres in the world. It hosts spectacular opera performances during the summer months.
