What to do?

Verona is a city that feels like it was built for daydreamers — a place where ancient stone arches frame gelato shops, and every corner seems to whisper a story. It's the city of Romeo and Juliet (yes, that balcony!), but it's also so much more than a love story. Verona doesn't try too hard — it just effortlessly charming.
