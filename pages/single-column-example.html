The Medieval Castle

Castelvecchio is a fortified medieval castle built in the 14th century by Cangrande II della Scala. The castle served as a military fortress and residence for the ruling Scaliger family.

Today it houses the Castelvecchio Museum, one of the most important museums in Verona, featuring a collection of medieval and Renaissance art.

The Bridge

The Scaliger Bridge connects the castle to the opposite bank of the Adige River. It was destroyed during World War II but has been faithfully reconstructed using the original materials recovered from the river.

Visiting Information

Hours & Tickets

The museum is open Tuesday through Sunday from 8:30 AM to 7:30 PM. It's closed on Mondays. Ticket prices vary, with discounts available for students and seniors.

The Verona Card includes admission to Castelvecchio and other major attractions in the city, making it a cost-effective option if you plan to visit multiple sites.
